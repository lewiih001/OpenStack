# ADR-0015: Keystone domain and project layout

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Deciders:** Lewis (lewiih001)
- **Phase:** 0 (Bootstrap), configured in Phase 2 and Phase 8

## Context

Keystone organises users and resources into domains and projects, and
quotas, roles and billing (CloudKitty) follow that structure. The cloud
will host internal platform services, Lewis's own products (Tek
Inventory, Tek Triangle clients) and, later, external customers.
Restructuring domains after resources exist means migrating every
resource and credential.

## Decision drivers

- Hard separation between customers (organisations)
- Delegated administration: an organisation manages its own projects and users
- Quotas and showback per product and environment
- Platform services isolated from tenants

## Options considered

1. **Single `Default` domain, one project per team**: simple, but no delegated administration, and project names collide across organisations.
2. **One domain per organisation, projects per team or product and environment.**
3. **Hierarchical projects (nested) inside one domain**: flexible, but quota and tooling support for nesting is uneven.

## Decision

- **One Keystone domain per organisation:**
  - `platform`: platform services and their service projects
  - `tek-inventory`: Tek Inventory
  - `tek-triangle`: Tek Triangle client work
  - one domain per future external customer
- **Projects per team or product and environment**, named per ADR-0012, for example `tek-inventory-prod`, `tek-inventory-stage` and `platform-svc-prod`.
- The `Default` domain holds only OpenStack service users created by Kolla. No people or tenant resources are placed there.
- Each organisation domain has a `manager` group (from Keycloak, ADR-0010) that can manage projects and role assignments inside that domain only.
- Every project gets explicit quotas through the OpenTofu `tenant` module. No project relies on default quotas.
- A `canary` project in the `platform` domain, with tight quotas, runs the synthetic checks.

A new tenant is a pull request adding one `tenant` module block in
`infra-live/prod/`. That block creates the domain or project, Keycloak group
mapping, quotas, network, router, security group, DNS zone and
application credential.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Customers are isolated at the domain level and can administer themselves.
- Quotas and CloudKitty showback map directly to products and environments.
- Onboarding is reviewed and repeatable.

### Negative

- Domain-scoped administration needs careful policy testing. Mitigation: policy tests in the lab for the `manager` role before the first external customer.
- More domains mean more mapping rules in Keycloak. Accepted; they are generated by the `tenant` module.

## Revisit when

- A customer needs nested projects for its own organisational structure.
- Keystone policy defaults change in a future SLURP release.
